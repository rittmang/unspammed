# Product Marketing Context — Unspammed

## Product and editions

- **Project/product**: Unspammed ([official repository](https://github.com/rittmang/unspammed)), formerly Watchtower.
- **Public marketing website**: [unspammed.rittmang.xyz](https://unspammed.rittmang.xyz), maintained in a separate private repository.
- **Category**: Community moderation workspace with a private owner dashboard and Telegram Mini App. Telegram is implemented today; Discord and Twitch are future connector work.
- **Hosted edition**: Unspammed Bot, [@unspammedbot](https://t.me/unspammedbot). The operator confirmed it running and ready for new groups on 2026-10-22; Get started opens its Telegram add-to-group flow. This is operator confirmation, not independent ingestion, billing or customer-provisioning verification. US$5/month remains a planning placeholder; do not advertise checkout, subscriptions or automatic provisioning.
- **Self-hosted edition**: Naaradmuni. Each installation brings its own Telegram bot, owner/group IDs, PostgreSQL, host and model-provider billing. The original owner's Naaradmuni bot remains a private self-hosted installation, never a default for others.
- **Public naming**: Use Unspammed throughout navigation, buttons, setup headings, metadata and illustrations. Primary marketing actions say Get started. Edition reference names do not become alternate public product branding.
- **Edition contract**: [docs/brand-and-editions.md](../docs/brand-and-editions.md). Use it for names, price status and implementation boundaries.

## Audience and job to be done

- **Primary audience**: Telegram community/supergroup owners and administrators who want less spam without losing control over legitimate conversation.
- **Core job**: Keep spam and repeat offenders out of the conversation without manual spam patrol. Review and model evaluation are optional advanced controls, not required marketing steps.
- **Self-hosted audience**: Operators able to maintain their bot, database, hosting and model account. The public bot targets administrators who prefer adding a bot to operating infrastructure.

## Product communication

1. New groups start in **Observe**: scores and explanations are saved without deletions, strikes or bans. Owners explicitly choose **Enforce** after reviewing behavior. Evaluate is a workflow stage, not a live moderation mode.
2. Lead public marketing with automatic protection, repeat-offender handling and owner control. Keep self-hosting secondary. Do not claim cross-platform connectors, organization roles, SaaS tenancy, subscriptions or provisioning that are not implemented.
3. State verified hosting evidence and platform limits plainly, including Render Free idle sleep and Vercel Hobby cron limits. Keep local tests, provider builds, deployed checks and physical Telegram-client checks separate. Never invent testimonials, customer counts, accuracy percentages or one-click deployment claims.
4. Preserve installation credentials, namespace identities and the existing Mini App experience. Display branding changes do not authorize live bot reconfiguration, policy approval, Enforce, secret rotation or billing changes.
5. Keep copy direct and layouts easy to scan. Avoid redundant captions, filler prose and decorative arrows; use simple symbols when they clarify an interaction.
