# Product Marketing Context — Watchtower

## Product & Category

- **Product**: Watchtower (`https://github.com/rittmang/watchtower`)
- **Public site**: `https://watchtower.rittmang.xyz`
- **Category**: Self-hosted Telegram moderation bot with a private owner dashboard and Telegram Mini App.

## Audience & Job to Be Done

- **Primary audience**: Telegram community/supergroup owners and administrators who started a group for real conversation and ended up acting as unpaid spam patrol.
- **Core Job to Be Done**: Keep the conversation worth having without manually policing scam links or risking false bans from opaque bots.

## Core Psychological Levers

1. **Default Effect & Regret Aversion**: Every new group starts in **Observe** (logs scores and explanations; zero deletions, strikes, or bans). Owners see exact decisions on their real group before enabling **Enforce**.
2. **Endowment & IKEA Effect**: Owners bring their own bot, PostgreSQL database, host, and model provider, and benchmark policies against their own human-labelled examples in the dashboard and Telegram Mini App.
3. **Pratfall Effect (Radical Honesty)**: State verified hosting evidence and real platform limits plainly (e.g., Render Free idle sleep, Vercel Hobby cron limits, Evaluate as a workflow stage rather than a live mode). Never invent testimonials, customer counts, accuracy percentages, or "one-click" claims.
4. **Cognitive Load & Hick's Law**: Let the layout and interactive exhibits do the explaining. Avoid secondary labels, redundant captions, filler descriptive prose, and decorative arrows; use clean Unicode symbols (`+`, `−`, `×`, `●`, `○`, `·`, `—`, `/`).
